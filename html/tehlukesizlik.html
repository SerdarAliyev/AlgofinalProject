Secure Watch Payments With the Chrono24 Escrow Service

Buy a watch from a professional dealer or private seller with added peace of mind. The Chrono24 Escrow Service holds your payment securely in an escrow account until your watch arrives. After delivery, your payment is held for 14 days when buying from a professional dealer, or up to 7 days when buying from a private seller, giving you time to inspect your watch before the funds are released.

14-Day Right of Withdrawal When Buying From Dealers

When you’re buying watches online, you want to see your watch in person before you commit. That’s why every new or pre-owned watch you buy from a dealer on Chrono24 comes with a 14-day right of withdrawal. Take your time: You have 14 days from delivery to decide if the watch is right for you. No reason needed: You can return your watch without explaining why. Purchases from private sellers aren’t covered by this right of withdrawal.

Returns Made Easy

Contact our support team: Send an email to our support team within 14 days of receiving the watch. They will confirm that your notice to return has been received on time and will initiate further steps.

Ship the watch back to the dealer - Send the watch back fully insured and in its original packaging. You will be responsible for the return shipping costs in most cases.

Receive a refund of the full purchase price - Finally, we will refund you the full purchase price and the initial shipping costs.

Commitment to Authenticity

Our promise: Every new and used watch purchased from a professional dealer on Chrono24 is authentic. Our dealers are required to verify each watch’s authenticity and may only list original timepieces. If concerns about authenticity arise, you have 14 days from delivery to contact our support team and get your money back. This promise applies exclusively to purchases from professional dealers.

What is the Chrono24 Escrow Service?

The Chrono24 Escrow Service offers both buyers and sellers more security when purchasing a watch. After the buyer has transferred the total amount to the escrow account, the money will be kept safe until the buyer has received the item and confirmed delivery. We usually trigger payout to the seller within 7 business days of the delivery confirmation. The payment can also be released earlier if the buyer agrees. Furthermore, using the Escrow Service allows us to offer you more support throughout the transaction process should something not go according to plan.

How can I pay for an order using an escrow service?

Many listings offer payment processing on Chrono24. In these cases, you transfer payment to a secure escrow account, where it is kept safe until you have the watch in your hands. The Chrono24 team is always available to help ensure your purchase runs smoothly. You can learn more about Chrono24 payment processing here: Buying from a commercial dealer. Buying from a private seller. If you're unable to process your payment using Chrono24's escrow account, please note the following: You can likely find other trustworthy escrow services in your own country. Make sure to research the escrow service thoroughly before making a final choice. Both buyer and seller must agree to the use of an escrow service. Please only accept information the escrow service provides directly to you. Do not rely on emails forwarded by the seller.

How much does it cost to make a purchase via the Escrow Service?

We charge a fee of 2.5% of the total price including shipping costs for use of the Escrow Service.

Can I return an order?

You have no legal right to a return when buying from a private seller. Each seller decides for themselves whether he or she accepts returns or not. If you want to return an item, please speak directly with the seller. Return details should be clarified in full before shipping any item back to the seller.

What is the return policy?

You should look into the dealer's return policy before making a purchase. Some dealers only accept returns on watches with their seals and packaging intact. It is in your best interest to fully insure the return shipment and to comply with the return deadline. We recommend reaching out to the dealer to discuss the process before returning the item.

What happens in case of a return?

If you've been charged a commission fee for a sale that resulted in a return, you will be credited the full amount of the fee. If the return is not yet visible in your Market Portal, please contact our support team.

What is the return policy?

When you place an order with Buyer Protection, you always have 14 days to return the item. If you pay the purchase price directly to the dealer, please look into their return policy beforehand. When making purchases with private sellers, you do not necessarily have the right to return your watch. However, you can still submit complaints to Chrono24 within 7 seven days of receiving your watch.

What is the Authenticity Guarantee?

Dealers are not allowed to list replicas or fakes on Chrono24 under any circumstances. The dealer is required to investigate the watch's authenticity before selling it and to list all known modifications in as much detail as possible in the item description. You can find additional details in our Authenticity Guarantee Terms & Conditions.

How do I place an ad for a watch?

Create a listing for your watch in just a few simple steps using our Listing Assistant: Enter your watch's reference number in the search bar. Our Listing Assistant will recognize the watch and fill out certain details automatically. Describe your watch in detail. The more detailed the description, the easier it is for potential buyers to find your listing. Add up to 10 images of the watch taken from different angles. Keep your camera handy; you'll be asked to take 2 additional photos of the watch set to specific times and upload them as proof of ownership. These photos are displayed in your listing. Register as a private seller by providing a copy of your valid photo ID.

Why Buying a Watch on Chrono24 Is Secure?

Chrono24 Buyer Protection is a package of security features that make buying a watch on Chrono24 safe and convenient. It doesn't matter whether you purchase your dream watch from one of the many vetted dealers or from a private seller. At the core of Chrono24 Buyer Protection is the Chrono24 Escrow Service, which ensures risk-free payment processing. Depending on whether you buy from a dealer or private seller, additional benefits may apply, such as a 14-day statutory right of withdrawal, insured shipping, and an Authenticity Guarantee.

How Payment via the Chrono24 Escrow Service Works?

Once you've found your dream watch on Chrono24 and placed a binding order, you'll receive a payment request. It asks you to transfer the amount due promptly to a Chrono24 escrow account. Once the payment has been received, the seller is notified and asked to arrange shipping. Once the watch arrives, you have time to check whether it matches the seller's description. If you bought the watch from a dealer, you have 14 days to inspect it. For purchases from a private seller, the period is 7 days. If everything meets your expectations, you can give Chrono24 approval to release payment to the seller. If the watch doesn't match the seller's description, inform Chrono24 within the inspection period, and you'll be refunded the purchase price.

Are watches insured during shipping?

When you buy a watch from a dealer on Chrono24, the shipment is always insured for its full value, regardless of whether the dealer is based domestically or abroad. If you buy from a private seller, you need to arrange the shipping details directly with the seller. Chrono24 strongly recommends insisting on sufficiently insured shipping with tracking. This lets you check the shipment status at any time and gives you better information in case the watch is lost or damaged in transit.
