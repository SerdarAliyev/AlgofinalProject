The Independent Watchmaker

MAEN - Proportion Over Presence

Neo-vintage aesthetics and integrated-bracelet cases, assembled in Switzerland with an emphasis on restraint and legibility.

Two friends in Stockholm, Sebastiaan Cortjaens and Jules van Helvoort, started MAEN in 2017 with a simple instinct: watches didn't need to keep getting bigger to feel substantial. Assembled in Switzerland and designed with real restraint, MAEN's Manhattan, Brooklyn, Hudson, and Skymaster collections borrow proportions from the sport watches of the 60s and 70s, built for people who'd rather wear something considered than something loud. Even the name carries that same instinct - MAEN is the old Dutch word for "moon," one of the oldest symbols of time there is. The brand has grown since without losing that original idea, staying true to design that trusts the wearer to notice the details instead of demanding they do.

Watches Worth a Closer Look

Manhattan 37 Ultra-Thin

An ultra-thin hand-wound movement in a compact 37-mm case - the model that best captures MAEN's whole design philosophy.

Hudson 38

A second time zone in the same restrained proportions, for collectors who travel but don't want a watch that shouts about it.

Skymaster 38 MKIII

MAEN's most technical piece, and the newest addition to a collection built on quiet confidence.
